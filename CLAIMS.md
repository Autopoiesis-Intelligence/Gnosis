## D — Physics / mathematical research tracks

The following must not be presented as established results of Gnosis without separate derivations and reproducible evidence:

- Born rule;
- Bell/nonlocal correlations;
- holographic information bounds;
- Lorentz invariance or LIV conclusions;
- Lyapunov monotonicity;
- alpha≈137;
- SU(2)×SU(3), E8 or other gauge-structure emergence;
- causal DAG structures as physical foundations;
- any universal theory-of-reality claim.

## E — Failed / rejected directions

The project has rejected the use of a permanent external correction operator as a fundamental requirement. It has also rejected treating human confirmation or an opaque global selector as a necessary part of the endogenous core.

Previous mathematical experiments that did not establish their intended theorem must remain classified as failed or inconclusive rather than promoted by repetition or narrative reinterpretation.

## Rule for promotion

A hypothesis can move upward in status only when the new evidence is explicit, reproducible and stronger than the evidence supporting its current classification.
